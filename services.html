Our services

We help you find weak spots, test your defenses, build a solid security plan, train your team, and protect your premises. Here's how each service works.

1

Vulnerability Assessment

Find the cracks before attackers do.

We check your systems, networks, and physical spaces for known weak spots, such as outdated software, weak configurations, unlocked restricted areas, and gaps in camera coverage. We show you where the vulnerabilities and weaknesses are, without exploiting them.

Why it matters

New weaknesses appear all the time. Regular checks catch them before someone else does.

What you get

  • A detailed and clear report with a prioritized list of issues
  • Regular scans and physical walkthroughs
  • A baseline to track your progress over time
2

Penetration Testing

Prove what an attacker could really do.

Our ethical hackers try to break into your networks, web applications, wireless systems, or even your people (through social engineering), the same way a real attacker would.

Why it matters

A list of weaknesses doesn't tell you how much damage an attacker could do. This test shows you.

What you get

  • A report with proof and clear steps to fix each issue
  • Retesting to confirm everything is closed
  • Evidence often needed for compliance, insurance, or client requirements
3

Cybersecurity Consulting

A smart security plan, built to meet the rules.

We help you design strong cyber and physical security from the ground up, and guide you through standards like ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, and NIST CSF.

Why it matters

Security built piece by piece leaves gaps, and regulations are complex. Falling short can mean fines, lost contracts, and lost trust.

What you get

  • A clear security design for your organization
  • Gap assessments, policies, and audit preparation
  • Ongoing guidance as regulations change
4

Security Awareness & Training

Turn your team into a human firewall.

We train everyone, from new hires to executives, to spot and respond to threats, both online (like phishing emails) and in person (like a stranger without a badge).

Why it matters

Most breaches start with a person, not a hacker. Informed employees are your first line of defense.

What you get

  • Simulated phishing emails with friendly coaching
  • Short, regular training tailored to each role
  • Practice drills for spotting and reporting suspicious activity
5

Surveillance & Access Control

Lock down your physical space.

We secure your buildings and assets with cameras, security doors, badge and biometric access, and visitor management.

Why it matters

Strong digital security means little if anyone can walk into a server room or leave with a laptop.

What you get

  • Only authorized people can enter sensitive areas
  • A clear record of who went where, and when
  • Faster investigation of theft or unauthorized access
